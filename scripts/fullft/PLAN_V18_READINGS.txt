================================================================================ Reading method from plan v18 on
(the user asked at 00:53 why registered readings keep coming back unsatisfying; Opus' answer, approved at 00:58)
1 Competing explanations first (at least two, plus "nothing"); for every experiment write what each predicts; run only
  experiments whose predictions differ. (T1's late side did not discriminate: every explanation predicted LATE HURTS.)
2 Measure the noise first: the same scenes again with other diffusion noise give the zero-effect band of each metric.
3 Effect size with a 95 % confidence interval against the smallest effect that matters (SESOI, set from what the paper
  needs), three outcomes only: clearly yes (the interval beyond the SESOI), clearly no (the interval inside it),
  uncertain (the interval crosses it) -> a pre-set extension (how many more episodes), written before the run.
4 Metrics close to the mechanism (extra presses, presses made with the full count in the ledger); success and other
  binary episode outcomes are descriptive.
5 Dose-response over a single cut-off where possible.
6 Zero-compute checks before an experiment is run: does the intervention move the quantity it is meant to move? (The
  delay decomposition showed T2b could shorten the record delay by about one frame only.)
Readings registered before 11-21 keep their words (T1, T2, O1, O3, swap lag, W1 / S1 / A1 texts); they are read with
this method's eyes in the reports, never re-judged.
